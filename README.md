
# The Olympic Games

An interactive Shiny app that takes a deep dive into 120 years of authentic Olympic Games data.
Breaking down different athletes, their respective countries and of course - the hardware!
We allow users to look into comparative metrics behind countries and when they have won the most as well as a map to view where the most medals are located.

### Tab Overview

#### Dashboard:

- A quick look into the number of all-time athletes, medals, countries and sports that have existed within the Olympic games, as well as,
age distribution, gender participation, top 10 countries and the top 10 athletes of all time.

#### Athletes:

- Pick whatever your favorite Olympic athlete is and view their performances all-time with medals and games they've participated in.

#### Countries:

- Compare your favorite countries within this tab with a chart to see their perspective performances over time, with a control for gender.

#### Sports:

- Analyze your favorite sports, where you can filter by gender and see the top athletes and countries within each event.

#### My Country:

- You will be brought to this page in the beginning after picking your favorite country, which will help you view all their medal statistics, top sports and athletes.

#### Dominance Index:

- Get a holistic view of the most dominant countries by medal and season within each decade since the birth of the Olympic Games.

#### Most Improved:

- Take a look into how different countries have improved decade over decade. 

#### Medal Map:

- A map which includes every country who has ever won a medal in the Olympic Games, where you can zoom and view how your favorite country stacks up against their geographical region.
